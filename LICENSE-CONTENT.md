# Content and image rights

The MIT licence in the repository LICENSE file applies to the software and custom code in this repository.

It does **not** grant a licence to photographs, logos, trademarks, or other third-party visual material.

## Photographs and other images

Instrument and facility photographs remain copyright of their respective photographer or creator. They are **not licensed for reuse** unless an image-specific record explicitly says otherwise.

Where a creator or copyright holder is known, the preferred notice is:

> © Person Name

If the repository does not yet record the individual creator, that missing attribution must not be interpreted as permission to reuse the image. The image remains copyrighted and permission is required.

The machine-readable image-rights source is `assets/image_rights.json`. The site build expands it to cover every shipped image. When a photographer or creator is confirmed, record the person's name there using a notice such as `© Person Name`; missing attribution is never interpreted as permission to reuse.

## Logos and trademarks

Names, logos, trademarks, and manufacturer marks remain subject to the rights of their respective owners. Their presence in this repository does not grant trademark or reuse rights.

## Structured instrument data and website text

Unless a separate licence notice says otherwise, factual instrument records and website text are made available for viewing through the public dashboard but are not relicensed by the MIT software licence.

# Microscope metadata questions for Slack

Prepared 13 September 2026. These are 23 separate draft messages for the 22 active microscopes and the retired SP5. Nothing has been posted. The retired synthetic Test Scope X1 was reviewed separately and needs no staff message. Questions reflect remaining gaps after the proposed corrections. Already recorded versions are not requested again.

## 1. 3i CSU-W1 Spinning Disk

```text
Hi everyone, please help us complete the AIC website. For the *3i CSU-W1 Spinning Disk*, could you help with the following?

1. What is the part number of the dichroic used for 730 nm imaging? Do we have its data sheet and the part number of the optic combining the NIR and visible lasers?
2. Could you share the data sheets for the CSU-W1 and widefield dichroics and the FF01-440/521/607/700 emission filter? The two spinning-disk records disagree on some bandwidths.
3. Which lamp or LED provides transmitted-light imaging, and how does light reach the camera or eyepieces? A simple sketch is enough.
4. Which LED modules are fitted in the X-Cite XLED1, and which laser and optics does the Vector FRAP unit use?
5. What GFP emission filter is currently installed? Our notes record a change from 525/30 to 525/50 on 18 August 2021; was this permanent, and were there later changes?

Screenshots of software information, accessible labels, data sheets or a simple sketch are all helpful. Please do not move or remove optics to answer these questions. Partial answers are welcome. Thanks!
```
